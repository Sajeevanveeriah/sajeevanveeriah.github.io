# Safety-And-Regulatory-Boundary

Open-Industrial-Automation is an engineering reference and simulation suite.

It is not:

- a safety PLC, safety instrumented system or emergency-stop circuit
- a machine-guard controller or safe-motion system
- a certified process-control platform
- an electrical design authority
- a validated electronic-records or electronic-signature platform
- a substitute for competent commissioning or physical verification

Any system that can affect people, product, equipment or the environment requires site-specific hazard analysis, independent protective functions, qualified hardware, secure architecture, controlled procedures, physical testing, commissioning and lifecycle maintenance.

The electronic-record and audit surfaces demonstrate readiness concepts only. An organisation must determine applicable rules, configure independent identity and audit services, validate the complete intended use and maintain procedural controls before relying on regulated records.
